# TechNova Sales & Profitability Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive two-page Power BI dashboard analysing sales performance, revenue, profitability, product performance, and customer segments. The project explores how sales channels, product categories, and discounting relate to business profitability.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall revenue, profit, profit margin, and order volume.
- Identify top-performing products by revenue and profitability.
- Compare revenue and profit across product categories.
- Evaluate performance across customer segments, sales channels, and regions.
- Identify potential opportunities to improve profitability.

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX measures and KPI calculations
- Data modelling and transformation
- Data visualisation and dashboard design
- Sales and profitability analysis
- Interactive slicers and filtering

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of business performance, including:
- Total revenue, total profit, profit margin, and total orders.
- Order status distribution.
- Revenue and profit by customer segment.
- Profit margin by customer segment.
- Sales channel and regional comparisons.
- Interactive filters for region, customer segment, product category, and sales channel.

### 2. Product & Profitability
Explores product-level performance through:
- Top five products by revenue.
- Revenue and profit comparisons across products.
- Revenue and profit by product category.
- Product revenue and profit margin comparisons.
- Top products by profit margin against the overall margin benchmark.

## Key Findings

- **Total revenue:** $6.37 billion.
- **Total profit:** $1.41 billion.
- **Overall profit margin:** 22.1%.
- **Total orders:** Approximately 10,000.
- Smartphones generated the highest revenue among the displayed product categories.
- The South West region generated the highest revenue among the displayed regions.
- The Logitech M185 Mouse recorded the highest displayed product profit margin at 44.3%.

## Business Recommendations

- Review discounting strategies to understand their effect on profit margins.
- Monitor product-level margins alongside revenue to identify profitable growth opportunities.
- Compare sales channel performance to inform channel investment decisions.
- Investigate regional and customer-segment differences to identify opportunities for improvement.
